What I do

From product thinking to
interface design.

A flexible design toolkit for moving from an early idea to a more considered product experience.

The process begins with understanding the business context, product objectives, stakeholder expectations, and the problems the product aims to solve. This involves reviewing project briefs, gathering functional requirements, analyzing existing workflows, and identifying user pain points that may affect the overall experience.

By breaking down business requirements into actionable design considerations, I establish a clear understanding of the product's scope, priorities, and expected outcomes. This foundation helps ensure that design decisions are driven by real needs, balancing user expectations, technical constraints, and business objectives rather than relying solely on visual preferences.

Once the requirements are defined, I translate them into structured user journeys, information architecture, and interaction flows that outline how users navigate through the product. This includes identifying entry points, defining decision paths, mapping system responses, and accounting for different scenarios, including empty states, errors, and alternative user actions.

I then develop low-fidelity wireframes to establish content hierarchy, screen layouts, and the placement of key interface elements. This stage allows the product structure and usability to be evaluated early, making it easier to identify gaps, refine workflows, and align the overall experience before investing time in detailed visual design.

With the product structure established, I transform wireframes into high-fidelity interfaces that communicate information clearly and support efficient user interactions. The process involves defining visual hierarchy, typography, color usage, spacing, layout composition, and interface states to create a cohesive experience across different screens and devices.

I also develop reusable components, design patterns, and interface guidelines to maintain consistency throughout the product. Beyond visual appearance, I consider usability, accessibility, responsive behavior, and interaction feedback to ensure that each interface element serves a clear purpose. The result is a scalable design foundation that supports both the immediate product requirements and future development.

I connect interface screens into interactive prototypes to demonstrate navigation, transitions, and expected system behavior across key user journeys. These prototypes help stakeholders review the proposed experience, identify usability issues, and provide feedback before development begins. When necessary, I refine the design based on validation findings and project feedback.

Before handoff, I organize design files, structure components, document interaction rules, define interface states, and clarify responsive behavior and relevant edge cases. I collaborate with developers to communicate design intent, resolve implementation questions, and ensure that the delivered product remains aligned with the approved design. The process continues through design reviews and implementation feedback to help maintain quality from design to release.
